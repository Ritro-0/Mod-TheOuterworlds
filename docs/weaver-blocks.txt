Weaver blocks
=============

A colony's gift score is one running total. It starts at 0 and never resets.
The first gift is offered at 32. After a gift, the next bar is the old bar
plus half of that bar, and never less than +32. With whole numbers that is:

  gift 1 at 32
  gift 2 at 64
  gift 3 at 96
  gift 4 at 144
  gift 5 at 216
  gift 6 at 324
  gift 7 at 486
  gift 8 at 729
  gift 9 at 1093

It is not double the previous score. Dumping a pile that jumps over several
bars still offers only one gift; the extra points count toward the bar after
that. There is a 15 minute wait between gifts (skipped if a baby was seen in
the last 10 seconds), and any harm pauses gifts for 10 seconds. A hostile
colony offers nothing. A player the colony does not trust is not handed a
gift even if the score is high enough.

Each stored block counts as one item. Points below are per block.


1. Blocks they value
--------------------

Only blocks in data/theouterworlds/tags/block/weaver_curiosities.json add
score, and only once a Weaver has put them in a colony net. Every copy is
kept and scored. The colony remembers the verdict, so later copies only get
a glance.

Precious, 8 points (item tag weaver_curiosity_precious):
  minecraft:beacon
  minecraft:budding_amethyst
  minecraft:diamond_block
  minecraft:emerald_block
  minecraft:netherite_block

Fine, 3 points (item tag weaver_curiosity_fine):
  every block in #minecraft:diamond_ores
  every block in #minecraft:emerald_ores
  minecraft:amethyst_block
  minecraft:amethyst_cluster
  minecraft:gold_block
  minecraft:lapis_block

Everything else in the curiosity tag, 1 point:
  every block in #minecraft:coal_ores
  every block in #minecraft:copper_ores
  every block in #minecraft:gold_ores
  every block in #minecraft:iron_ores
  every block in #minecraft:lapis_ores
  every block in #minecraft:redstone_ores
  every block in #minecraft:candles
  every block in #minecraft:flowers
  minecraft:copper_block
  minecraft:glowstone
  minecraft:iron_block
  minecraft:redstone_block
  minecraft:sea_lantern
  minecraft:shroomlight
  minecraft:soul_lantern
  minecraft:torch

A block that is on both the fine list and the precious list scores 8.
Outerworld ores are not in this tag, so they are not worth points.


2. Blocks they find interesting and keep once
----------------------------------------------

These are specimens. The colony keeps one of each block, in a net, and
leaves further copies of that same block where they are. They add no score.
If that one copy is no longer in a loaded net, they may pick up another.

A specimen is a block whose id path is exactly one of these:
  dirt
  coarse_dirt
  rooted_dirt
  podzol
  mycelium
  mud
  clay
  gravel
  sand
  red_sand
  sandstone
  red_sandstone
  stone
  cobblestone
  deepslate
  cobbled_deepslate
  tuff
  calcite
  dripstone_block
  andesite
  diorite
  granite
  netherrack
  blackstone
  basalt
  smooth_basalt
  end_stone
  soul_sand
  soul_soil
  anorthosite
  norite
  gabbro
  enstatite
  komatiite
  pyroxenite
  anhydrite
  oxidized_basalt
  sulfuric_basalt

or any block whose id path ends in "_ore".

Not specimens, even if the name looks close:
  anything in the curiosity tag above (those are valued instead)
  Anchor parts (tholin fibre, home plate, weaver pad, weaver net,
    straw bed, iron chain, lantern, tholin thread, tholin opal reflector)
  Amberworld ground they already live with (tholin, tholinic regolith,
    liquid methane, packed ice, bedrock, air, any liquid)
  a path containing tholin, stairs, slab, wall, brick, polished,
    chiseled, farmland, or suspicious

So vanilla diamond ore is worth 3, not a specimen. A block whose id ends in
_ore, such as anorthosite_copper_ore or oxidized_basalt_opal_ore, is a
specimen: kept once, 0 points. raw_hematite, raw_jarosite, and
raw_perchlorate do not end in _ore and are not on the name list, so they
are thrown and score nothing.
Plain stone is kept once. Stone bricks, stairs, and slabs are not.


3. Blocks they use to furnish their pods
-----------------------------------------

There is no fixed list. Any block that opens a screen counts: chests,
barrels, furnaces, crafting tables, and anything else with a menu.

One Weaver keeps one of each block type. They place it on a solid floor
in their own pod, within 5 blocks of the bed, not on the bed, the home
plate, a pad, or a net. A second copy of a type that Weaver already
placed is thrown off the Anchor. Another Weaver in the same colony can
still keep one of that type for their own pod.

Screen blocks are not stored in nets and add no score. If that Weaver
has no free spot in the pod, the block is thrown instead.


What they ignore or throw
--------------------------

Anchor parts and Amberworld ground are left alone. Anything that is not
a valued block, a specimen, or a screen block is thrown off the Anchor
after the first look. Later copies of a thrown block only get a glance.

Blocks placed by a player the colony does not trust are always thrown.
They are not scored, kept, or furnished.
